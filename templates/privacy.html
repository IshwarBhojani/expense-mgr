Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only the information necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data — basic logs such as login timestamps, used only for security and debugging.

We do not collect payment information, precise location data, or any data from your device beyond what you enter directly.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate Spendly and provide you with the service:

  • To display your expense history, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To respond to support requests you initiate.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the core functionality of the app.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database accessible only to you through your authenticated account. We apply standard security practices to protect your data from unauthorised access, alteration, or loss.

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Upon deletion, your data is permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with any third-party services, advertisers, or analytics providers.

If we integrate any third-party tools in the future (for example, for hosting or error tracking), we will update this policy and notify users in advance. Any such services will be bound by strict data processing agreements.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent to data processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the data we hold, please reach out through the contact information provided in the application.

We are committed to addressing your concerns promptly and transparently.
